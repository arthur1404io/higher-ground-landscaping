Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in Oakland Township, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping serves Oakland Township's rural-suburban properties with the same attention to craftsmanship we've brought to Oakland County for 29 years. Larger acreage, natural landscapes, and ambitious outdoor living projects are what we do best -- and Oakland Township gives us the space to do it.

Landscaping for Oakland Township's Spacious Properties

Oakland Township stands apart from the rest of Oakland County. Where Birmingham and Royal Oak measure lots in fractions of an acre, Oakland Township properties in the 48306 and 48363 zip codes routinely span two, five, even ten or more acres. The township's commitment to preserving its rural-suburban character -- large lot minimums, protected open spaces, and the natural beauty of Paint Creek flowing through the heart of the community -- creates a setting where landscape work takes on a different scale and philosophy entirely.

On these larger properties, the goal isn't to landscape every inch. It's to create defined outdoor living zones near the home that transition naturally into the surrounding woodland, meadow, or agricultural land. A thoughtfully designed paver patio and outdoor kitchen adjacent to the house, transitioning through a stone walkway and native plantings to a fire pit area at the edge of a tree line -- that's the kind of project that makes Oakland Township living special. We understand how to create that boundary between cultivated and natural in a way that feels organic rather than abrupt.

Paint Creek Corridor and Natural Drainage

Paint Creek runs through Oakland Township, and properties along or near the creek face specific drainage and erosion challenges. The creek corridor has a defined floodplain, and properties within it need drainage solutions that account for periodic high water events. Even properties above the floodplain deal with the rolling topography and clay soil common to the area. On larger lots, water can travel significant distances across the surface before reaching a low point -- and when it arrives, it arrives with force. We engineer drainage systems for Oakland Township properties that manage water volume over distance, using strategically placed catch basins, extended French drain runs, and dry creek beds that serve both functional and aesthetic purposes.

Ambitious Outdoor Living on Acreage

The generous lot sizes in Oakland Township allow for outdoor living projects that simply aren't possible in more densely developed communities. Full outdoor kitchens with stone countertops and built-in grills, large fire pit gathering areas that seat twelve or more, expansive paver patios that flow into pool deck areas, and multi-level retaining wall systems that create usable terraces on sloped terrain. We've built all of these across Oakland Township, often incorporating natural fieldstone and boulders sourced locally that tie the constructed elements to the natural landscape.

Long driveways are another hallmark of Oakland Township properties, and many homeowners here invest in landscape lighting that illuminates the drive from road to house -- both for safety and for the dramatic arrival experience it creates. Combined with accent lighting on the home's facade and key landscape features, a well-lit Oakland Township property makes a statement that matches the investment these homes represent. Tony and the Higher Ground team bring the same precision to a five-acre Oakland Township estate that we bring to a quarter-acre Birmingham lot -- the scale changes, but the craftsmanship never does.
