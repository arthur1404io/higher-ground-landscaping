Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in Huntington Woods, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping has served Huntington Woods for 29 years, helping families in the "City of Homes" create functional, beautiful outdoor spaces that make the most of every square foot. Tony Barash and his team understand that in this tight-knit community, your yard is as much a gathering place as your living room.

The "City of Homes" Deserves Expert Landscaping

Huntington Woods earned its nickname for good reason. This small, closely bounded community -- just over one square mile with roughly 6,300 residents in the 48070 zip code -- is almost entirely residential. There's no commercial district to speak of, no large-scale development. Just homes, families, tree-canopied streets, and a community pride that shows in how residents maintain their properties. The Detroit Zoo borders the community to the south, Burton Elementary and the rec department anchor neighborhood life, and word travels fast about which contractors deliver quality work and which ones don't.

The homes in Huntington Woods are predominantly well-kept colonials, bungalows, and ranch-style houses built between the 1920s and 1960s. Lots are modest -- typically 50 to 70 feet wide -- which means outdoor design needs to be intentional about space. A paver patio that's too large overwhelms the yard. A drainage system that's poorly routed takes up precious side yard real estate. We design for Huntington Woods' proportions specifically: compact but not cramped, functional but not utilitarian. The result is outdoor spaces that feel like a natural extension of the home.

Family-Centered Outdoor Living

Huntington Woods is a family town. Kids ride bikes on the sidewalks, neighbors gather on patios, and backyards see constant use from spring through fall. The outdoor living spaces we build here reflect that lifestyle. Durable paver patios that stand up to years of foot traffic, kids, and Michigan weather. Fire pits positioned for evening gatherings where families can roast marshmallows and neighbors can drop by. Low-maintenance landscape beds that look great without demanding weekend gardening time from busy parents. Walkways and steps that are safe for kids and lit properly for nighttime.

Drainage in a Mature, Dense Community

The drainage dynamics in Huntington Woods are shaped by its density and age. Homes are close together, many properties share grade lines, and the original drainage infrastructure is 60 to 80 years old. When one property changes its grading or adds an impervious surface -- a new driveway, patio, or addition -- it can redirect water onto a neighbor's lot. We navigate these situations carefully, designing drainage solutions that protect your property without creating problems next door. French drains along foundation walls, underground downspout extensions that reach the street, and regrading that restores positive drainage are all common solutions we install in Huntington Woods.

The mature tree canopy throughout Huntington Woods is one of the community's defining features, but it also means heavy root systems that compete with drainage installations and shade conditions that affect lawn health and plant selection. Tony's understanding of tree root zones and shade-tolerant landscape design ensures that our work complements the existing canopy rather than damaging it. For Huntington Woods homeowners who value their community and want outdoor spaces that match, Higher Ground is the contractor your neighbors already trust.
