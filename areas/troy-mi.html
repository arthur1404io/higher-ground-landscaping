Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in Troy, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping has been serving Troy homeowners and businesses since 1996. With 29 years of experience across Oakland County, Tony Barash and his team specialize in the hardscape replacements, residential drainage corrections, and landscape upgrades that Troy properties need as their original installations age.

Troy's Unique Landscaping Needs

Troy is Oakland County's largest city by population and one of its most diverse in terms of housing stock. The subdivisions that define Troy -- neighborhoods like Northfield Hills, Somerset Park, and the developments along Wattles and Square Lake Roads -- were largely built during the construction boom of the 1980s and 1990s. That means many Troy homes are now 30 to 40 years old, and the original concrete driveways, builder-grade patios, and basic landscaping installed at construction are reaching the end of their lifespan.

This creates both a challenge and an opportunity. The challenge: aging concrete cracks, heaves, and crumbles under Michigan's freeze-thaw punishment. The original drainage -- if it was installed at all -- often fails as soil settles over decades. Basements that stayed dry for 20 years suddenly start taking water. The opportunity: replacing outdated hardscapes with modern paver systems that are both more attractive and more durable, while simultaneously fixing underlying drainage issues that the original builders ignored. Across Troy's 48083, 48084, 48085, and 48098 zip codes, we've handled hundreds of these aging-subdivision upgrades.

Residential Drainage in Troy Subdivisions

Troy's relatively flat terrain and clay-heavy soil create a specific drainage profile. Unlike the rolling hills of Bloomfield or Rochester, Troy properties tend to have gentle grades that don't naturally move water away from structures. Builder-installed drain tile around foundations often clogs or collapses after two to three decades. We see this constantly in Troy: homeowners who've lived in their house for years without issues suddenly dealing with water intrusion because the original perimeter drain failed. Our approach combines French drain installation with proper regrading to create positive drainage away from the foundation -- a permanent fix rather than a Band-Aid.

Commercial and Mixed-Use Properties

Troy's Big Beaver Road corridor and the commercial areas near Somerset Collection create demand for professional landscape maintenance and hardscape installation beyond the residential market. Office parks, retail plazas, and mixed-use developments along Rochester Road and Livernois need landscape contractors who understand commercial requirements -- ADA-compliant walkways, proper stormwater management, and aesthetics that attract tenants and customers. While our primary focus is residential, we've completed commercial hardscape and drainage projects throughout Troy's business districts when the scope matches our expertise.

For Troy homeowners looking to increase property value before selling, or simply to modernize a home they plan to stay in, we typically recommend starting with drainage assessment and correction, then moving to hardscape replacement. A new paver driveway and patio installed over properly prepared base with integrated drainage will outlast the next 30 years of Michigan weather -- and it transforms the entire look of a 1980s-era home.
