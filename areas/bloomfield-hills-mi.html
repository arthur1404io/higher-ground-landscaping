Estate Landscaping for Bloomfield Hills Properties

Bloomfield Hills consistently ranks among the wealthiest cities in the United States, and the properties here reflect that distinction. Sprawling estates along Woodward Avenue, Long Lake Road, and Lahser Road sit on multi-acre lots with mature oak, maple, and beech canopies that have been growing for generations. These landscapes demand a contractor who can work at scale -- managing comprehensive drainage across rolling terrain, designing plantings that complement century-old trees, and building hardscapes that match the architectural grandeur of the homes themselves.

The country club communities that define Bloomfield Hills -- Oakland Hills, Bloomfield Hills Country Club, and the neighborhoods surrounding Cranbrook -- have exacting aesthetic standards. Homeowners in these areas don't just want functional landscaping; they need cohesive outdoor environments that integrate with their property's existing character. We've spent nearly three decades earning trust in these neighborhoods, handling everything from full-property landscape renovations on five-acre estates to targeted drainage corrections around pools and tennis courts in the 48301, 48302, and 48304 zip codes.

Drainage Challenges on Large Bloomfield Hills Properties

Oakland County's heavy clay subsoil creates drainage problems that intensify with property size. On a quarter-acre lot, you might manage with a single French drain line. On a two-acre Bloomfield Hills estate, you need a comprehensive water management system -- multiple drain runs, catch basins at low points, proper swales directing runoff away from structures, and often a connection to the municipal storm system. The rolling topography that makes Bloomfield Hills properties so beautiful also creates natural collection points where water accumulates after heavy rains or snowmelt. We engineer systems that work with the natural grade rather than fighting it, preserving the property's character while eliminating standing water and foundation threats.

Comprehensive Estate Services

Our Bloomfield Hills projects often involve coordinating multiple services into a single cohesive plan. A typical estate engagement might include regrading the rear yard, installing a tiered natural stone retaining wall system, building a paver patio with an outdoor kitchen and fireplace, adding landscape lighting along the driveway and garden beds, and integrating drainage throughout. We handle the full scope because these elements need to work together -- the retaining wall affects drainage patterns, the patio grade needs to shed water properly, and lighting placement depends on the final landscape layout.

Mature tree preservation is another priority unique to Bloomfield Hills. When we're working near established trees -- some of which are 80 to 100 years old -- we take precautions to protect root zones, maintain proper soil levels, and ensure drainage changes don't redirect water away from trees that depend on it. Tony's horticultural background from Michigan State gives him the understanding to balance hardscape construction with the living landscape.

Every Bloomfield Hills project uses premium materials: Unilock pavers with lifetime warranties, natural fieldstone sourced from Michigan quarries, and commercial-grade drainage components designed for longevity. These properties deserve nothing less, and our reputation in this community depends on work that still looks flawless a decade later.
