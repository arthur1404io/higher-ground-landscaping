Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in West Bloomfield, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping brings 29 years of Oakland County experience to West Bloomfield's lakefront communities and large-lot properties. From managing water flow on properties near Cass Lake and Orchard Lake to building expansive outdoor living areas, Tony Barash and his team understand the specialized needs of waterfront and lake-adjacent homes.

Lakefront and Waterfront Landscaping Specialists

West Bloomfield is defined by its lakes. Cass Lake, Pine Lake, Orchard Lake, Walnut Lake, and dozens of smaller bodies of water create a community where many homeowners live on or near the water. This proximity brings unique landscaping challenges that inland properties simply don't face. Fluctuating water tables, shoreline erosion, runoff management that must protect lake water quality, and the need for landscapes that look stunning from both the street side and the water side all require specialized knowledge.

Waterfront drainage in West Bloomfield's 48322, 48323, 48324, and 48325 zip codes is fundamentally different from drainage on a standard subdivision lot. The water table on lakefront properties can be just a few feet below grade, which limits how deep you can install drainage systems and affects what foundation protection methods work. When it rains, the soil is already saturated from the high water table, so surface drainage becomes even more critical. We design systems that move water efficiently across the surface to designated discharge points, using swales, channel drains, and gravity-fed pipe systems that account for the unique hydrology of lake-adjacent parcels.

Outdoor Living on West Bloomfield's Large Lots

The generous lot sizes throughout West Bloomfield -- many properties span half an acre to several acres -- give us room to design truly expansive outdoor living environments. Multi-zone paver patios with dedicated spaces for dining, lounging, and cooking. Pool deck installations that integrate seamlessly with the surrounding landscape. Fire pit areas positioned to capture lake views. Retaining walls that create usable terraces on properties that slope down toward the water. These are the kinds of ambitious projects we love, and West Bloomfield properties are perfectly suited for them.

Protecting Your Lake and Your Property

West Bloomfield homeowners -- especially those on the lake associations for Cass Lake, Pine Lake, and Orchard Lake -- are deeply invested in protecting water quality. That's why our approach to lakefront landscaping always considers runoff impact. Permeable paver systems that allow rainwater to filter through rather than sheet directly into the lake, rain gardens positioned to capture and filter runoff from driveways and patios, and proper grading that prevents erosion while maintaining natural shoreline buffer zones. We build landscapes that enhance your property and protect the lake environment that makes West Bloomfield special.

Whether you're on one of West Bloomfield's premier lakefront lots or in one of the established neighborhoods along Middlebelt, Orchard Lake Road, or Lone Pine, Higher Ground delivers the same attention to drainage engineering and design quality that has made us Oakland County's go-to landscape contractor for nearly three decades.
