Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in Birmingham, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping has been Birmingham's trusted landscape contractor since 1996. For 29 years, Tony Barash and his crew have helped Birmingham homeowners transform their properties with expert drainage solutions, custom paver installations, and stunning landscape designs built to last through Michigan's demanding seasons.

Why Birmingham Homeowners Choose Higher Ground

Birmingham is one of Oakland County's most desirable communities, known for its walkable downtown, tree-lined streets, and beautifully maintained historic homes. But the charm of Birmingham's older properties -- many built in the 1920s through 1950s -- comes with unique landscaping challenges that require a contractor who truly understands the area.

The tight lot lines throughout neighborhoods like Quarton Lake Estates, Poppleton Park, and the streets near Pierce Elementary mean drainage must be handled with precision. Water has nowhere to go on a 50-foot-wide lot unless you engineer it correctly. Oakland County's heavy clay soil compounds the issue -- rainfall doesn't percolate naturally, and without proper grading and French drain systems, water pools against foundations, saturates lawns, and creates ice hazards in winter. We've solved hundreds of these drainage puzzles across Birmingham's 48009 and 48012 zip codes, often in situations where other contractors said there was no fix.

Birmingham's walkable downtown and high property values make curb appeal a genuine investment. A well-designed paver driveway or front walkway in Birmingham doesn't just look good -- it adds real resale value in a market where buyers expect polished exteriors. Our luxury paver driveways are among the most requested projects in the city, using Unilock materials backed by a lifetime guarantee to withstand Michigan's brutal freeze-thaw cycles. From Maple Road to Lincoln, you'll find our work throughout Birmingham's most prominent streetscapes.

Common Birmingham Projects

The types of projects we handle most frequently in Birmingham reflect the city's unique character. Historic home renovations often need updated foundation drainage before any cosmetic landscaping begins. Homeowners along Eton Road and Southfield Road regularly hire us for complete front yard transformations -- removing aging concrete driveways and replacing them with permeable pavers that solve drainage problems while dramatically improving appearance. Backyard entertaining spaces are another Birmingham staple: with smaller lots, every square foot matters, and we design patios, fire pits, and outdoor kitchens that maximize usable space without feeling cramped.

Landscape lighting is particularly popular here. Birmingham's evening culture -- residents walking downtown for dinner, strolling through neighborhoods on summer nights -- means well-lit properties stand out. Our LED lighting systems highlight architectural details, illuminate walkways for safety, and create ambiance that extends your outdoor living season well past sunset.

Built for Michigan Weather

Every project we install in Birmingham is engineered for Southeast Michigan's climate. That means proper base depths for pavers, drainage integrated into every hardscape, and plant selections that thrive through zone 6 winters. We don't cut corners on preparation -- because in Michigan, shortcuts show up as heaved pavers and flooded basements within two years. Tony personally inspects every Birmingham project before we call it complete.
