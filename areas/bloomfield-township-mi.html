Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in Bloomfield Township, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping has served Bloomfield Township homeowners for 29 years, specializing in refreshing mature landscapes, correcting long-standing drainage problems, and modernizing hardscapes on the large residential lots that make this community one of Oakland County's most desirable addresses.

Refreshing Bloomfield Township's Mature Landscapes

Bloomfield Township occupies a geographic and cultural middle ground between the compact elegance of Birmingham to the south and the grand estates of Bloomfield Hills to the north. The township's large residential lots -- typically a half-acre to over an acre throughout the 48301, 48302, 48303, and 48304 zip codes -- feature mature landscapes that were often planted 30 to 50 years ago. These properties have tremendous character: towering shade trees, established shrub borders, and a settled, gracious feel that newer developments simply can't replicate.

But maturity has its challenges. Overgrown foundation plantings that obscure windows and trap moisture against the house. Shade from decades of tree growth that has killed the lawn underneath. Aging concrete patios and walkways that have cracked and settled. And drainage systems -- if they were installed originally -- that have deteriorated underground where nobody could see the decline until water started appearing in the basement. We specialize in the kind of thoughtful renovation these Bloomfield Township properties need: updating and modernizing while preserving the mature elements that give the property its value and appeal.

The Cranbrook Area and Surrounding Neighborhoods

The neighborhoods surrounding the Cranbrook Educational Community represent some of Bloomfield Township's most prestigious addresses. Properties along Lone Pine Road, Cranbrook Road, and Woodward Avenue carry a certain expectation of landscape quality. Homeowners here invest in their outdoor spaces not just for personal enjoyment but because these neighborhoods set the standard for the broader community. Our work in the Cranbrook area ranges from complete property renovations -- removing tired overgrown landscapes and replacing them with contemporary designs that honor the architectural style of the home -- to targeted projects like boulder retaining walls along sloped side yards or paver motor courts that replace failing asphalt.

Solving Decades-Old Drainage Problems

Many Bloomfield Township homes were built during an era when residential drainage received less engineering attention than it does today. Builders relied on simple grading and basic drain tile that was expected to function indefinitely. After 30 to 40 years, that assumption breaks down. Clay soil has shifted and compacted, changing surface grades. Tree roots have infiltrated and crushed drain tile. Additions, pool installations, and landscape changes have altered how water flows across the property. We've diagnosed and solved drainage failures throughout Bloomfield Township that other contractors misidentified. Tony's ability to read a property's topography and understand where water is coming from -- not just where it's showing up -- is what separates a permanent solution from a temporary patch.

If your Bloomfield Township property needs a landscape refresh, a drainage overhaul, or a complete outdoor transformation, Higher Ground brings the experience and the craftsmanship these properties deserve. We understand what it means to work on homes in this community, and we deliver accordingly.
