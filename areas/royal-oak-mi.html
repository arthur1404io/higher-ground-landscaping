Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in Royal Oak, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping has served Royal Oak homeowners for 29 years, specializing in the creative outdoor designs and smart drainage solutions that this vibrant, walkable community demands. When every square foot of your yard counts, you need a contractor who knows how to maximize it.

Making the Most of Royal Oak's Compact Lots

Royal Oak is one of Oakland County's trendiest communities -- a city where younger homeowners are investing seriously in their properties and outdoor spaces have become extensions of indoor living. But Royal Oak's charm comes with a spatial reality: lots here are typically 40 to 60 feet wide, and backyards are measured in hundreds of square feet rather than fractions of an acre. That constraint is exactly why hiring a landscape contractor with design expertise matters more here than almost anywhere else in the county.

We've built dozens of patios, outdoor kitchens, and entertainment areas in Royal Oak's 48067, 48068, and 48073 zip codes that feel spacious despite the compact dimensions. The key is thoughtful layout: a well-designed 12-by-16-foot paver patio with a built-in seating wall and fire pit can become a genuine outdoor room that you use from April through October. Pair that with strategic landscape lighting and plantings that create privacy from neighbors ten feet away, and a small Royal Oak backyard becomes one of the best rooms in the house.

Drainage on Tight Lots

Royal Oak's smaller lots create concentrated drainage challenges. When your property is narrow and surrounded by neighbors on all sides, there's limited room for water to spread out and absorb. Oakland County's clay soil makes it worse -- rainfall sheets off hard surfaces and sits on the lawn because it can't percolate through the clay. Basements in Royal Oak's older bungalows and colonials (many built in the 1920s through 1940s) are especially vulnerable. We design compact drainage systems that fit within tight side yards, channel water to the street or storm system, and protect foundations without eating up the limited outdoor space you have. Underground downspout extensions, channel drains along driveways, and slim-profile French drains along property lines are common solutions we deploy here.

Curb Appeal in a Walkable City

Royal Oak's pedestrian culture -- people walk to restaurants on Main Street, stroll through neighborhoods for exercise, bike to parks -- means your front yard gets noticed. A crisp paver walkway, well-designed front landscape beds, and accent lighting create the kind of curb appeal that stands out on streets where neighbors are paying attention. This is a community that takes pride in its homes, and the right landscape investment signals that. We've worked on homes along Campbell, Vinsetta, and throughout the neighborhoods between 11 Mile and 14 Mile that showcase how even modest front yard upgrades make a dramatic difference when the craftsmanship is right.

For Royal Oak homeowners weighing whether to renovate their outdoor space, the math is straightforward: in a market where homes sell fast and buyers expect move-in-ready, a well-designed patio and drainage-corrected yard deliver strong ROI. Tony and the Higher Ground team will work within your budget and your lot constraints to find the smartest use of your space.
