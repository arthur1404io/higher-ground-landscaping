Expert Landscaping & Drainage Services in Franklin, MI

Higher Ground Landscaping has served the Village of Franklin for 29 years, bringing expert craftsmanship to a community that values natural beauty, historic character, and understated elegance. Tony Barash and his team understand that Franklin's aesthetic standards are among the highest in Oakland County -- and our work reflects that.

Preserving Franklin's Historic Village Character

Franklin is unlike anywhere else in Oakland County. This historic village in the 48025 zip code has deliberately maintained its rural character for over 180 years, resisting the commercial development and subdivision growth that transformed neighboring communities. The result is a place of winding roads, no streetlights by design, split-rail fences, stone walls, horse farms, and homes set back on wooded lots that feel like they belong in the New England countryside rather than suburban Detroit. Landscaping here isn't about making a bold statement -- it's about enhancing what's already there while respecting the village's deeply held aesthetic values.

Franklin's architectural standards and community expectations mean that materials matter enormously. Natural stone is strongly preferred over manufactured block. Fieldstone walls, Pennsylvania bluestone walkways, and native Michigan boulders integrate with the village's character in ways that stamped concrete or bright-colored pavers never could. Landscape lighting must be subtle -- downlit, warm-toned, and positioned to illuminate without creating the urban glow that Franklin intentionally avoids. We source natural stone from Michigan quarries, select warm-color-temperature LED fixtures, and design installations that look like they've always been part of the property.

Equestrian Properties and Large Acreage

Franklin has a strong equestrian heritage, and many properties along Franklin Road, Scenic Highway, and the surrounding lanes maintain horse facilities, paddocks, and riding trails. Landscaping on these properties requires understanding how outdoor spaces interact with equestrian use -- proper fencing integration, drainage that keeps riding areas dry, walkways and patios positioned away from barn traffic, and plantings that are safe around horses. We've worked on several Franklin equestrian properties, designing outdoor living areas that complement rather than conflict with the agricultural elements that make these properties special.

Drainage in Franklin's Wooded Terrain

Franklin's terrain is characterized by dense tree cover, rolling grades, and the Franklin branch of the Rouge River running through parts of the village. Properties here deal with shade-heavy conditions that prevent grass growth, root systems that complicate excavation, and natural water courses that need to be managed without altering the landscape character. Our drainage work in Franklin prioritizes solutions that look natural: dry creek beds lined with native stone rather than plastic catch basins, subtle grading changes that redirect water without obvious earthwork, and rain gardens planted with native species that absorb runoff while adding seasonal color.

For Franklin homeowners who want landscape work done right -- work that respects the village's character, uses authentic materials, and is executed with the precision that these properties demand -- Higher Ground has been the trusted choice for nearly three decades. Tony personally oversees every Franklin project because the margin for error in this community is zero. The village may be small, but the standards are as high as anywhere in Michigan.
