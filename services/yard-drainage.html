Drainage Systems

What We Install

Every drainage system we build is tailored to your property's specific water management needs.

French Drain Installation & Repair

French drains are the most common solution for redirecting groundwater away from foundations and low-lying areas. We install 4-inch perforated pipe in a gravel-filled trench lined with geotextile fabric. Our French drains use clean washed stone -- never recycled concrete -- to prevent clogging and ensure 20-30 year performance in Michigan's conditions. French drains in Oakland County typically cost $1,500 to $5,000 depending on length and depth.

Catch Basin Systems

Catch basins collect surface water at low points in your yard and channel it to a discharge point. We install 9x9, 12x12, and larger basins with proper grating, connected by solid PVC pipe at the correct grade. Catch basins are ideal for areas where water pools on the surface after rain, especially around patios, driveways, and walkways.

Site Grading & Re-grading

Sometimes the best drainage solution is reshaping the land itself. Proper grading ensures water flows away from your foundation at a minimum slope of 1 inch per foot for the first 6 feet. We use laser levels and grade stakes to achieve precise, uniform slopes that move water where it needs to go without creating new problems downstream.

Foundation Drainage

Water against your foundation causes basement leaks, cracks, and structural damage. We install interior and exterior foundation drains, waterproof membrane systems, and proper grading to keep water away from your home. Foundation drainage is especially critical in Oakland County where clay soils hold moisture against basement walls for extended periods.

Underground Downspout Extensions

Your roof collects thousands of gallons of water per year. If your downspouts dump that water at your foundation, you are creating a drainage problem. We connect downspouts to underground solid pipe that carries water 15-30 feet from the house to a pop-up emitter or dry well. This single improvement often solves persistent basement moisture issues.

Channel Drains & Dry Creek Beds

Channel drains (also called trench drains) intercept sheet flow across driveways and patios. Dry creek beds serve as both a functional drainage swale and a beautiful landscape feature, moving water naturally across your property. We build dry creek beds with river rock, boulders, and native plantings that look intentional, not like a construction project.

Local Expertise

Why Michigan Drainage Is Different

Clay Soil Challenges

Oakland County sits on heavy clay soil that drains 50 to 100 times slower than sandy soil. Water pools on the surface and saturates the ground around your foundation. Every drainage system we install accounts for clay's low permeability with oversized gravel beds and strategic discharge points.

Freeze-Thaw Cycles

Michigan averages 30-40 freeze-thaw cycles per winter. Each cycle expands and contracts the soil, shifting pipes and disrupting poorly installed drains. We bury pipe below the frost line where possible and use flexible couplings at transitions to prevent cracking and separation.

Spring Runoff Volume

Michigan's spring snowmelt combined with April showers dumps enormous amounts of water in a short period. Our drainage systems are sized for peak flow, not average rainfall. We design for the worst-case scenario so your yard handles every spring without flooding.

Common Questions

Yard Drainage FAQ

How much does yard drainage cost in Michigan?

Yard drainage costs in Michigan typically range from $1,500 for a simple French drain to $15,000 or more for a complete property drainage system. The cost depends on the severity of the water problem, the size of the area, soil conditions, and the type of drainage system required. Higher Ground Landscaping provides on-site assessments and detailed estimates.

What are the signs I need yard drainage in Oakland County?

Common signs you need yard drainage include standing water after rain for more than 24 hours, a soggy or spongy lawn, water pooling near your foundation, basement leaks or moisture, erosion along slopes, mold or mildew smells in your basement, and gutters overflowing due to poor grading. Michigan's clay-heavy soils and freeze-thaw cycles make drainage problems especially common in Oakland County.

What types of drainage systems does Higher Ground install?

We install French drains, catch basin systems, site grading and re-grading, foundation drainage, underground downspout extensions, sump pump discharge lines, channel drains for driveways and patios, and dry creek beds and swales. Tony Barash has 29 years of experience solving Michigan's toughest drainage problems.

How long does a French drain last in Michigan?

A properly installed French drain in Michigan lasts 20 to 30 years or more. The key is correct installation with the right gravel, pipe grade, and filter fabric to prevent clogging. Higher Ground uses heavy-duty perforated pipe, clean washed stone, and geotextile fabric designed for Michigan's clay soils and freeze-thaw conditions.

Can yard drainage be installed in winter in Michigan?

Yard drainage installation in Michigan is best done from April through November when the ground is not frozen. However, early spring is the ideal time to plan and schedule your drainage project so the system is in place before heavy spring rains. Contact Higher Ground for a winter consultation to get on the spring schedule.
