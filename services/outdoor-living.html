Custom outdoor kitchens, fireplaces, fire pits & entertainment areas. Serving Oakland County since 1996.

Premium Service

Custom Outdoor Living Spaces in Oakland County, MI

Outdoor living spaces in Oakland County cost between $8,000 for a simple fire pit and seating area to $75,000 or more for a full outdoor kitchen with fireplace, built-in grill, and covered structure. Higher Ground Landscaping designs and builds custom outdoor rooms that extend your home's living area into the backyard, engineered to withstand Michigan's four-season climate.

Michigan homeowners get roughly 6 months of prime outdoor living from April through October, and a well-designed outdoor space maximizes every day of it. A stone fireplace or fire pit extends the season into November and starts it in March. An outdoor kitchen eliminates the back-and-forth trips inside during summer entertaining. Pergolas and shade structures make July afternoons comfortable instead of sweltering.

Tony Barash has built outdoor living spaces across Birmingham, Bloomfield Hills, Troy, Rochester Hills, and throughout Oakland County since 1996. Every project uses cultured stone, natural stone, and premium materials rated for Michigan's freeze-thaw conditions. We handle all gas line connections, electrical rough-ins, and drainage integration as part of the build.

What We Build

Outdoor Living Features

Outdoor Kitchens & Grilling Stations

Our outdoor kitchens range from simple built-in grill islands ($8,000 to $15,000) to full-featured kitchens with refrigerators, sinks, storage, countertops, and bar seating ($25,000 to $50,000+). We build using concrete block cores clad in cultured or natural stone veneer, with granite or bluestone countertops. Every kitchen includes proper drainage, gas connections, and electrical for lighting and appliances.

Stone Fireplaces & Fire Pits

An outdoor fireplace is the centerpiece of any backyard. We build freestanding fireplaces from $12,000 to $30,000 with natural or cultured stone finishes, wood-burning or gas inserts, and optional pizza oven additions. Fire pits range from $3,000 to $8,000 and can be wood-burning or gas with electronic ignition. Both options extend your outdoor season by months.

Seating Walls & Benches

Seating walls define spaces, provide extra seating for entertaining, and add architectural interest. We build them from natural stone, segmental block, or cultured stone to match your patio and other structures. Seat walls are typically 18 to 20 inches tall with a comfortable cap stone on top. They cost $80 to $150 per linear foot installed.

Pergolas & Shade Structures

Pergolas provide filtered shade, define outdoor rooms, and support climbing plants like wisteria and clematis. We build cedar and pressure-treated pergolas from $5,000 to $15,000 depending on size and style. For full shade, we can add retractable canopies or louvered roof systems. Pergolas also serve as a framework for string lights and outdoor fans.

Cultured Stone Veneers

Cultured stone veneer transforms plain concrete block, poured walls, and existing structures into beautiful stone features. We use Eldorado Stone, Boral, and other premium manufactured stone products that look identical to natural stone at a lower cost and weight. Applications include fireplace surrounds, kitchen islands, house foundations, and accent walls.

Outdoor Bars & Entertainment Areas

Custom outdoor bars with granite tops, built-in coolers, and under-counter storage create the ultimate entertainment space. We integrate bars into kitchen islands or build standalone bar structures with seating for 4 to 8 guests. Add a mounted TV, sound system rough-in, and landscape lighting for a complete outdoor entertainment experience.

Michigan-Built

Engineered for Michigan Weather

Freeze-Thaw Rated Materials

Every material we use is rated for Michigan's freeze-thaw cycles. Our concrete block cores use moisture-resistant mixes. Stone veneers are installed with proper weep systems and flashing. Countertops are sealed against moisture penetration. Nothing we build will crack, spall, or delaminate from winter exposure.

Proper Drainage Integration

Outdoor structures need drainage just like indoor ones. We grade surfaces away from structures, install drain channels around kitchens and fireplaces, and ensure water never pools against stone veneer. Standing water against masonry in Michigan causes efflorescence, spalling, and structural damage within a few winters.

Gas & Electrical Coordination

We coordinate with licensed gas fitters and electricians to run gas lines for grills, fireplaces, and fire pits, plus electrical for lighting, outlets, and appliances. All connections are properly sleeved and buried, with shutoff valves accessible for seasonal winterization.

Common Questions

Outdoor Living FAQ

How much does an outdoor kitchen cost in Michigan?

Outdoor kitchens in Michigan range from $8,000 for a basic built-in grill island to $50,000 or more for a full kitchen with refrigerator, sink, storage, countertops, and bar seating. Higher Ground provides free design consultations and detailed estimates.

Can outdoor fireplaces be used year-round in Michigan?

Yes. Outdoor fireplaces and fire pits can be used throughout Michigan's cooler months. A well-built stone fireplace generates significant radiant heat that keeps a seating area comfortable even in temperatures down to the 30s. Gas fireplaces with electronic ignition are the easiest to use in cold weather.

What stone is best for outdoor living spaces in Michigan?

We recommend cultured stone veneer (like Eldorado Stone) for vertical surfaces and natural bluestone or granite for countertops and caps. These materials withstand freeze-thaw cycles, resist moisture damage, and maintain their appearance for decades.

How long does it take to build an outdoor kitchen?

Most outdoor kitchen projects take 2 to 4 weeks from start to finish. Simple grill islands can be completed in about one week. Full outdoor kitchens with fireplaces, seating walls, and pergolas may take 4 to 6 weeks.

Do I need a permit for an outdoor fireplace in Oakland County?

Permit requirements vary by municipality within Oakland County. Most cities require a building permit for permanent outdoor fireplaces and gas-connected fire pits. Higher Ground handles the permit process for you. Portable fire pits typically do not require permits but may have setback requirements.
