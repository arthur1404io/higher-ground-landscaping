Water Features

Custom ponds, waterfalls, fountains, and water walls that add tranquility to any outdoor space. Serving Oakland County since 1996.

Specialty Service

Custom Water Features in Oakland County, MI

Water features in Oakland County cost between $3,000 for a bubbling rock fountain to $25,000 or more for a natural stone pond with waterfall. Higher Ground Landscaping designs and builds custom ponds, waterfalls, fountains, water walls, and pondless systems that add the soothing sounds of moving water and create a focal point in your outdoor space.

The sound of flowing water transforms any outdoor space. It masks neighborhood noise, creates a calming atmosphere for relaxation, and attracts birds and wildlife to your yard. A well-designed water feature becomes the centerpiece of your landscape -- the element that draws the eye and makes your property unique.

Tony Barash has built water features across Oakland County since 1996, working with natural stone, boulders, and modern materials to create features that look like they belong in your landscape. Every water feature includes a recirculating pump system, proper basin or reservoir, and winterization provisions for Michigan's freezing temperatures.

Water Feature Types

What We Build

Natural Stone Waterfalls

Natural stone waterfalls create dramatic visual and audio impact. We stack boulders and flagstone to create cascading water that looks natural. Small waterfalls cost $5,000 to $8,000. Large multi-tier cascades run $15,000 to $25,000. Every waterfall includes a hidden basin, recirculating pump, and plumbing.

Backyard Ponds & Koi Ponds

Ponds create an entire ecosystem in your backyard. We build ponds from 50 to 500+ square feet with rubber liners, rock edges, aquatic plantings, and biological filtration. Koi ponds require additional depth (minimum 3 feet for Michigan winters) and multi-stage filtration. Ponds cost $5,000 to $20,000+.

Pondless Waterfall Systems

Pondless waterfalls provide sound and beauty without an open pond -- ideal for families with small children or homeowners wanting minimal maintenance. Water recirculates from a hidden underground reservoir. Pondless systems cost $3,000 to $10,000.

Modern Water Walls

Water walls create a sleek, contemporary look with water flowing down a vertical surface. We build them from natural stone slabs, stainless steel, copper, and glass panels. Sizes range from 3-foot accent pieces to 8-foot statement features. Water walls cost $5,000 to $15,000.

Bubbling Rock Fountains

Bubbling rocks are the simplest and most affordable water feature. A drilled boulder sits on a hidden basin with water bubbling up through the center. They take up minimal space and require almost no maintenance. Costs start at $3,000 installed.

Stream & Creek Features

Meandering streams create natural flowing water through your landscape. We build streams with rubber liner, natural stone, gravel beds, and native plantings along the banks. Streams incorporate small drops, bends, and wider pool areas for visual interest.

Michigan Considerations

Water Features in Michigan's Climate

Winterization

Every feature includes easy winterization provisions. Pumps are removed before the first hard freeze. Fish ponds get de-icers and aeration systems. Pondless systems simply have their pump disconnected -- the underground reservoir is frost-safe by design.

Energy Efficiency

Modern recirculating pumps draw 200 to 400 watts -- about the same as a few light bulbs. Monthly operating costs run $10 to $25. We size pumps for the right flow rate without wasting energy.

Year-Round Beauty

Water features look stunning in every season. Spring brings returning flow, summer is peak enjoyment, fall creates reflections of changing foliage, and winter transforms waterfalls into ice sculptures. Some homeowners run their waterfall year-round for the ice formations.

Common Questions

Water Features FAQ

How much does a water feature cost in Michigan?

Costs range from $3,000 for a bubbling rock fountain to $25,000+ for a natural pond with waterfall. Pondless waterfalls cost $3,000 to $10,000. Garden ponds cost $5,000 to $15,000. Koi ponds cost $10,000 to $20,000+.

How do you winterize a water feature in Michigan?

Pumps are removed or drained before the first hard freeze. Fish ponds get de-icers and aerators. Pondless reservoirs freeze naturally and are frost-safe by design. We offer winterization services.

Do water features attract mosquitoes?

No. Moving water does not attract mosquitoes -- only stagnant water does. All our features use recirculating pumps. Ponds with fish add another layer of protection since fish eat mosquito larvae.

How much maintenance does a pond require?

A well-designed pond requires about 30 minutes per week during the active season. Pondless features and fountains need even less -- just occasional debris removal and annual pump cleaning.
