Brick & Stone Paver Installation

Premium paver patios, driveways, and walkways built to withstand Michigan winters. Unilock lifetime guarantee.

Core Service

Expert Brick Paver Installation in Oakland County, MI

Brick paver installation in Oakland County costs between $15 and $35 per square foot, with most patio projects ranging from $4,500 to $15,000. Higher Ground Landscaping builds premium paver patios, driveways, walkways, and pool decks using Unilock, Belgard, and natural stone materials engineered to survive Michigan's brutal freeze-thaw cycles for decades.

The difference between pavers that last 5 years and pavers that last 50 years is what happens below the surface. We install 6 to 8 inches of compacted Class II gravel base over geotextile fabric, then 1 inch of leveling sand, before a single paver is placed. This foundation prevents frost heaving, settling, and shifting that destroys cheaper installations within a few Michigan winters.

Tony Barash and his crew have installed hundreds of paver projects across Birmingham, Bloomfield Hills, Troy, Rochester Hills, and every corner of Oakland County since 1996. We use polymeric sand in every joint to lock pavers together, prevent weed growth, and resist ant intrusion. Our installations come with Unilock's lifetime guarantee on the product and our own workmanship warranty.

Paver Services

What We Build

From intimate garden patios to grand motor courts, we design and build paver projects of every scale.

Paver Patios & Entertainment Areas

A paver patio is the foundation of your outdoor living space. We build patios from 100 to 2,000+ square feet with integrated seating walls, fire pit pads, and outdoor kitchen foundations. Standard paver patios in Oakland County range from $4,500 to $15,000. Raised patios with retaining wall borders cost more but create dramatic visual separation from the lawn. We help you choose colors, patterns, and borders that complement your home's architecture.

Paver Driveways & Motor Courts

A paver driveway transforms your home's curb appeal instantly. We install driveways using 2-3/8 inch thick pavers rated for vehicular traffic with a minimum 8-inch compacted gravel base. Paver driveways in Birmingham and Bloomfield Hills typically cost $20,000 to $50,000 depending on size and material choice. Herringbone patterns provide the strongest interlock for vehicle traffic. We also install paver aprons and borders around existing concrete or asphalt driveways for a more affordable upgrade.

Walkways & Garden Paths

Paver walkways connect your driveway to your front door, your patio to your pool, or your garden areas with safe, level surfaces. We install both formal geometric walkways with soldier course borders and natural meandering paths using flagstone or irregular pavers. Walkway projects typically run $2,000 to $8,000 depending on length and material.

Paver Steps & Staircases

Grade changes need steps, and paver steps must be structurally engineered for safety and longevity. We build freestanding and integrated stair systems with proper footings, risers, and non-slip treads. Every set of steps includes drainage provisions so water does not pool on the landing or erode the base material behind the stairs.

Paver Repair & Restoration

Existing pavers settling, shifting, or growing weeds? We provide complete paver restoration including removing and re-leveling sunken sections, replacing damaged pavers, re-sanding joints with polymeric sand, and sealing surfaces. Restoration costs a fraction of replacement and can make a 15-year-old patio look brand new. We also match existing pavers when additions are needed.

Bluestone, Flagstone & Natural Stone

For a more organic, high-end look, we install natural stone patios and walkways using bluestone, flagstone, travertine, and Michigan fieldstone. Natural stone costs more than manufactured pavers but offers unique character and timeless beauty. We set natural stone on both mortar beds and dry-laid gravel bases depending on the application and your aesthetic preference.

Built to Last

Why Our Base Preparation Matters

6-8" Compacted Gravel Base

We excavate to the proper depth and install 6 to 8 inches of compacted Class II gravel in 2-inch lifts. Each lift is compacted with a plate compactor to 95% or better. This is double what many contractors install and the reason our pavers stay level for decades, not years.

Geotextile Fabric Separation

Geotextile fabric between the subgrade soil and the gravel base prevents clay from migrating up into the gravel and compromising drainage. In Oakland County's heavy clay soils, this step is essential. Without it, the base becomes saturated clay within a few years and pavers begin to heave.

Polymeric Sand & Edge Restraints

Polymeric sand in the joints hardens when wet, locking pavers together and preventing weed growth and insect intrusion. Aluminum or concrete edge restraints along all borders prevent lateral spreading. Together, these elements create a unified surface that acts as a single structure rather than individual loose pieces.

Common Questions

Brick Paver FAQ

How much do brick pavers cost in Michigan?

Brick paver installation in Michigan costs between $15 and $35 per square foot installed, depending on the paver type, pattern complexity, and base preparation required. A typical 300 sq ft patio costs $4,500 to $10,500. Paver driveways cost more due to the heavier base required for vehicle traffic, typically $20,000 to $50,000 for a full driveway.

How long do brick pavers last in Michigan winters?

Properly installed brick pavers last 25 to 50 years in Michigan, even through harsh winters. The key is proper base preparation -- 6 to 8 inches of compacted gravel base with geotextile fabric underneath. This prevents frost heaving and settling.

What is the best paver material for Michigan driveways?

For Michigan driveways, we recommend Unilock or Belgard interlocking concrete pavers rated for vehicular traffic. These are 2-3/8 inches thick with interlocking patterns that distribute weight evenly. A herringbone pattern provides the strongest interlock against shifting.

Do pavers increase home value in Oakland County?

Yes, paver installations typically return 60 to 75 percent of their cost in home value. In Oakland County's competitive real estate market, a well-designed paver patio or driveway is a significant selling point.

Does Higher Ground offer a warranty on paver installation?

Yes. We are an authorized Unilock installer, so our installations come with Unilock's lifetime guarantee on the product plus our own workmanship warranty. If pavers settle or shift due to installation issues, we come back and fix it at no charge.
